🧠 What is OmniJev?

OmniJev is a revolutionary omni-modal AI model that understands your questions about images, videos, screenshots, and robot scenes — then answers with calibrated probabilities. Unlike traditional chatbots that generate text, OmniJev directly evaluates your question and gives you a precise probability score, meaning faster, more accurate, and more reliable responses.

Whether you're a researcher, developer, or curious enthusiast, OmniJev brings state-of-the-art multi-modal understanding to your fingertips — with a single forward pass and zero generated tokens.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🤔 Is OmniJev free to use?

Yes! OmniJev is open-source under the Apache-2.0 license. You can download, use, and even modify it for your own projects.

🔒 Do I need an internet connection?

No, once installed, OmniJev runs entirely on your local machine. Your data stays with you.

🎓 Do I need programming skills?

Not at all! OmniJev comes with a friendly graphical interface. If you can ask a question, you can use OmniJev.

OmniJev v1.1 is available in Small, Medium, and Large configurations, allowing you to choose the perfect balance between speed and accuracy for your hardware.
